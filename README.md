# Sonic — Velocity

A complete browser fan game with three levels, three playable characters, momentum-based running, double jumps, dash attacks, collectible rings, and a three-phase Eggman boss.

Open `index.html` in a modern browser and select **LET’S RUN**. No build, installation, or server is needed. Optional Google Fonts enhance the typography; the game and all artwork run locally without them.

| Control | Action |
| --- | --- |
| A / D or ← / → | Run |
| Space / W / ↑ | Jump; press again for a double jump |
| X / J | Dash attack; automatically targets nearby enemies |
| Shift + movement | Super-speed boost |
| Escape / P | Pause and resume |
| 1 | Sonic: fastest running and boost |
| 2 | Tails: double jump, then hold jump to fly |
| 3 | Knuckles: double jump, then hold jump to glide; dash punches deal double damage |

Switch characters anytime with the number keys (or numpad) or the on-screen character buttons. Switching preserves your rings, lives, energy, position, and jump count. Tails' flight and Knuckles' glide consume energy. All three characters can complete every level.

Touch devices have movement, boost, dash, and jump buttons, plus the character selector. The music-note button enables synthesized sound effects.

Play through **Emerald Coast**, **Sky Ruins**, and **Iron Fortress** before reaching Eggman. Each level requires all three blue power cells to unlock its exit. Follow the cell-distance hint and search above the main path. Collected cells stay collected after a lost life.

The coast has spike traps; the ruins add gaps, moving platforms, and crushing blocks; the fortress adds timed laser barriers and armored robots. Red warnings signal an impending hazard. Jump over spikes and pits and time your movement through the crushers and lasers. Falling into a pit costs a life even if you have rings. Silver armored robots take two ordinary attacks or one Knuckles punch.

Complete an act to carry your rings forward and earn an extra life (up to five). Each act has its own halfway checkpoint. Restarting begins a fresh three-act campaign with your selected character.

Rings protect you from a hit. Pick up scattered rings before they disappear. Getting hit with no rings costs a life and returns you to a checkpoint. Boost energy recharges when released and when collecting rings.

Eggman waits behind the final gate in Iron Fortress. He has 14 hit points and three increasingly fast phases. Move away from red missile targets, jump over ground shockwaves, and use a jump followed by a dash when his shield turns green. The dash homes in within 400 world units. Knuckles deals two damage per successful punch. The arena checkpoint restores 15 rings after losing a life, and restarts the boss fight. Your fastest three-act victory is saved in this browser when local storage is available, separately from the original one-level record.

Built with HTML, CSS, and Canvas 2D. Original procedural scenery and character illustrations; no game assets are downloaded. This is an unofficial fan game, not affiliated with Sega.
